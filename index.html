About

01 / Profile

I’m Haram Jung, Digital Media at Ajou University, Class of 2026. I’m working toward becoming a game designer, and also drawn to services that solve real problems.

Over the past year I built projects with Claude across three hackathons and shipped a service on campus, and saw firsthand how LLMs dismantle the barriers to building.

A single creation that gathers every art humankind has made.
That, I believe, is exactly what a game is.

I want to build with sincerity and passion, always!
